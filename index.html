What is ISRLATA?

Not only ideas.
Action that can be seen.

ISRLATA means Integrated and Sustainable Real Life African Transformation Action. It is a proposed, community-driven system for turning useful ideas, local experience and shared responsibility into practical action.

Its approach begins with real people and everyday life: how we grow food, care for water and land, use local materials, protect culture, guide one another and create opportunities for future generations.

This is an evolving initiative. Activities, partnerships, results and proposals will be clearly identified as they are developed and verified.
